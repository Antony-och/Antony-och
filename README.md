## About Me

I'm a full-stack developer passionate about building **useful software, thoughtful interfaces, and systems that solve real business problems.**

My work spans the entire product lifecycle — from UI/UX design and frontend development to backend architecture, database engineering, integrations, and deployment.

I enjoy turning complex workflows into simple, intuitive experiences, especially for businesses looking to modernize how they operate.

- **Product engineering:** Building scalable web applications and digital platforms.
- **Interface design:** Creating clean, responsive, user-focused experiences.
- **Systems architecture:** Developing APIs, database structures, and business logic.
- **Business automation:** Streamlining commerce, payments, inventory, and operations.
- **Local impact:** Exploring practical technology solutions for Kenya and East Africa.

> My philosophy: great software should look good, work reliably, and make people's lives easier.
